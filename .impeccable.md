# SAMAP — Design Context

## Users

**Primary audience: Prospective members researching health coverage in Paraguay.**

- Age 25–55, urban Asunción and Greater Asunción, middle-to-upper middle class
- Researching for themselves or for their family (spouse, children, elderly parents)
- Navigating a significant financial decision — monthly premiums are a real budget line
- Context of use: evenings at home or lunch breaks at work, browsing on phone or desktop
- Emotional state: **anxious optimism** — they want health security but fear choosing wrong or overpaying
- **Not tech-savvy necessarily** — this is healthcare, not a SaaS product. They expect clarity, not cleverness.

**Secondary audience: Referrals and word-of-mouth.** Existing members recommending SAMAP to family. They already trust the Sanatorio Adventista brand; the site should reinforce that trust, not try to reinvent it.

---

## Brand Personality

**Three words: Warm. Reliable. Grounded.**

- Warm: This is about family health — the interface should feel human, caring, approachable. Not clinical cold, not startup-casual.
- Reliable: 35 years, 8,000+ members, institutional backing (IASD). This is a decision people trust with their lives. The design should communicate permanence and solidity.
- Grounded: No gimmicks, no hype. Real coverage, real pricing, real contact. Paraguayan sensibility — direct, honest, no-nonsense.

**Tone:** Warm professional. Think: a trusted family doctor who explains things clearly and doesn't upsell you. NOT: a startup landing page, NOT: a government bureaucracy form, NOT: a generic hospital site.

**Anti-references:** Avoid looking like:
- A generic SaaS pricing page (intercom-style cards, "starting at $X/mo", neon accents)
- A hospital info site (sterile, institutional, all-blue, stock handshake photos)
- A fintech app (dark mode, glowing gradients, "revolutionizing health")
- A pharmacy chain ad (sale badges, urgency timers, discount percentages)

---

## Aesthetic Direction

**Theme: Light.** Healthcare on mobile, late at night, by anxious family members. Light theme is the correct choice here.

**Palette:**
- Institutional blue `#274767` — primary, authority, medical trust (used sparingly: key headings, nav, CTA backgrounds)
- Sea green `#6CA3AB` — SAMAP's differentiating color, used for bands, accents, active states, secondary CTAs
- Dark neutral `#2F2E2D` — body text, grounded and readable, not harsh black
- Backgrounds: warm off-whites, subtle tints. No cold grays.

**Typography direction (TBD — do not use reflex defaults):**
- Display: something with warmth and character — avoid "designy" feel. Consider a humanist or optical-size face. No Inter, Roboto, or Open Sans.
- Body: clean, highly readable at small sizes, generous x-height for Guaraní/Spanish diacritics legibility
- Scale: strong contrast between display (large, fluid) and body. Not a flat 8-step scale.

**Visual language:**
- Photography: real SAMAP Sanatorio environments, staff warmth, family-friendly settings. NO generic stock hospital photos.
- Icons: line-weight consistent, humanist style, not outlined-heavy technical style
- Layout: left-aligned text, asymmetric compositions, generous whitespace, varied rhythm — NOT centered everything on cards
- Decorative: subtle, purposeful. The institutional colors and photography do the heavy lifting — no graphic clutter.

**What makes this unforgettable:** The Sanatorio Adventista connection. 35 years of institutional trust, the IASD brand halo, real people at Pettirossi 380. The site should feel like walking into a well-run clinic — warm, organized, reassuring. No run-of-the-mill medicina prepaga template.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** Plans, prices, and coverage are the product. Every design decision serves comprehension first.

2. **Trust through honesty.** Real pricing (no "desde" with hidden fees), honest coverage descriptions, clear contact info. Do not use countdown timers, fake urgency, or discount badges.

3. **Human warmth, not clinical cold.** Photography shows real care. Copy speaks to the person, not the patient number. Color palette warms the institutional blue.

4. **Mobile-first, always.** Paraguay mobile penetration is high. Lead forms and plan comparisons must work flawlessly on small screens.

5. **Conversion through confidence, not pressure.** CTAs are clear and inviting ("Cotizar mi plan", "Llamanos", "Escribinos por WhatsApp"). The goal is to lower the barrier to contact, not to force a decision.

## Key Pages & Jobs-to-Be-Done

| Page | Visitor's Job |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Understand who SAMAP is, feel confident in 35 years of trust, see that plans exist |
| **Planes** | Compare plans, understand coverage, see pricing in Gs., decide which to explore |
| **Cotizador** | Answer a few questions, get a personalized plan recommendation + estimated price |
| **Prestadores** | Find a doctor or clinic near them in Asunción or beyond |
| **Contacto** | Get in touch via form, WhatsApp, or phone — low friction |
| **Blog** | Educate themselves, build trust, see SAMAP as a thought leader in health |

## Competitive Landscape (Paraguay)

- **PREMISA** — warm copy, "a tu medida", family focus, plans from Gs. 240,000, strong institutional feel
- **KEMI Salud** — modern, bilingual (es/pt), coverage also in Argentina, pricing from Gs. 120,000, tech-forward tone
- **Midoctor** — tiered plan names (Ejecutivo, VIP), lifestyle-framing ("si priorizas la tranquilidad"), clean but slightly corporate
- **Asismed** — digital-first features (portal, app, telemedicina), group enterprise focus, institutional backing
- **Salud Protegida** — family-first copy ("tu familia es lo más importante"), emotional but restrained
- **Medilife** — preventive health framing, "Pasos que Suman" campaign, community feel

**SAMAP Differentiation:** The Sanatorio Adventista institution itself. Direct hospital access, IASD medical standards, 35-year track record, real sanatorio physical presence at Pettirossi 380. No other prepaga in Paraguay has this exact institutional halo.

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Border-left colored stripes on cards (Ban 1 from impeccable guidelines)
- Gradient text (Ban 2 from impeccable guidelines)
- Gradient hero backgrounds
- Centered text-everything layouts
- Identical-size card grids
- Glassmorphism or blur effects
- Fake "4.8★ from 2,300 reviews" social proof widgets
- Urgency timers or "limited spots" pressure tactics
- Dark mode default
- Inter, Roboto, or Open Sans body fonts
- Fraunces, Playfair Display, or DM Sans display fonts
